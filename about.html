About Nutryx

Leading the way in personalized nutrition and holistic health transformation.

Our Journey

Science Meets Personalization

At Nutryx, we are a team of dedicated nutrition experts passionate about promoting healthy lifestyles through personalized diet plans. Our mission is to simplify the journey to better health by providing practical, effective, and enjoyable nutrition strategies tailored to your unique needs.

Founded by alumni of the prestigious ICMR - National Institute of Nutrition, Nutryx is a trailblazer in personalized nutrition. Backed by decades of expertise, we specialize in clinical and sports nutrition that empowers individuals to unlock their full potential.

Our Expertise

Lifestyle Diseases

Addressing conditions such as diabetes, thyroid issues, and PCOS with precision clinical nutrition.
